Our Story

In the quiet coastal town of Nagapattinam, where the palms whisper secrets to the sea, Pearl Palm Resort was born from a dream — not of grandeur, but of peace. In 1999, Mr. R. Thiraviyam, a retired sailor and nature lover, returned to his hometown after two decades of life at sea. Inspired by the memories of his childhood spent by the shore, he decided to build a place where people could rest, reconnect, and rediscover the calm that only the ocean can offer. What began as three handmade huts and a beachfront kitchen slowly evolved into a warm and welcoming retreat. By 2008, the resort grew to include eco-cottages, hammocks under the palms, and a garden-to-table restaurant serving the freshest South Indian seafood. Today, Pearl Palm Resort is known for its sunrise rooms, coconut grove spa, and cultural bonfire nights. Generations of travelers have called it their second home — a place where luxury feels like simplicity, and every sunset feels like a blessing. Still managed by the Thiraviyam family, our resort is a tribute to the land, the sea, and the stories they hold.
